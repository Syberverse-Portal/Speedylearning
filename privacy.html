How SpeedyLearning uses information

For grown-ups: This is a summary of the website's technical privacy design, not a substitute for a complete privacy notice. The operator must add their identity, contact details, lawful basis and retention policy before wider launch.

Progress stays on this device

Learning scores, practice mistakes, names you choose to add, and preferences are saved in your browser on this device. They are not automatically uploaded to a SpeedyLearning account. A grown-up can reset learning progress using My Progress.

Visits and optional activity totals

If the optional visitor service is enabled, the site records anonymous times when its homepage is opened so the operator can understand overall traffic. It does not save names, children's answers, browser fingerprints or precise locations in that visit database. Anonymous subject activity counts are switched off by default and only sent when enabled in device settings. These event records are intended to be retained for up to 30 days.

AI help is optional

The learning helper uses a built-in school glossary first. An optional AI helper may be enabled in settings by a grown-up when the server has been configured. Questions typed to the helper are then sent to the AI service to generate a reply, but aren't saved to learner profiles by SpeedyLearning. Do not type a name, address, school, phone number or other private information into the helper.

External resources

Some pictures and fonts are loaded from third-party websites, which can receive ordinary web requests. Offline lessons only cache SpeedyLearning's own educational files.

Your choices

Settings let you turn optional learning recommendations, anonymous activity totals and AI assistance on or off. To remove local learning records, a grown-up can use the progress reset on this device or clear this site's browser storage.
