How SpeedyLearning uses information

For grown-ups: This is a summary of the website's technical privacy design, not a substitute for a complete privacy notice. The operator must add their identity, contact details, lawful basis and retention policy before wider launch.

Logins need no email

Each child makes a login with a username and a secret 4-number PIN, and picks a buddy character and their school year. There is no email address, real name or password. We suggest a made-up username (the site can suggest one) rather than a child's full name.

The PIN is never stored as typed: SpeedyLearning keeps only a scrambled (salted and hashed) version. After 5 wrong PINs the login pauses for 15 minutes, and longer if wrong PINs continue. When a login is made, a grown-ups' recovery code is shown once; it is the only way to set a new PIN if one is forgotten, so please write it down or print the login card. On a shared device, each child types their PIN to switch to their own progress.

Where progress is saved

When a child is logged in, their learning progress (answers and scores, practice mistakes, streaks, finished activities, journey and quest progress, buddy and school year) is saved in this browser and copied to their SpeedyLearning login, so they can carry on on another device. It is stored by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, in its European Union data region. Logins nobody uses for 12 months are deleted automatically, with all their progress.

To stop people guessing PINs, the number of login attempts from each network address is counted for one minute. That address is not saved.

A grown-up can change a PIN, log a child out of one device (their progress stays saved online), or delete a login and all its progress straight away, from Grown-ups: manage logins (deleting needs the child's PIN). Children can also play without logging in; that progress stays only on the device. A grown-up can reset learning progress from My Progress.

Visits and optional activity totals

If the optional visitor service is enabled, the site records anonymous times when its homepage is opened so the operator can understand overall traffic. It does not save names, children's answers, browser fingerprints or precise locations in that visit database. Anonymous subject activity counts are switched off by default and only sent when enabled in device settings. These event records are intended to be retained for up to 30 days.

AI help is optional

The learning helper uses a built-in school glossary first. An optional AI helper may be enabled in settings by a grown-up when the server has been configured. Questions typed to the helper are then sent to the AI service to generate a reply, but aren't saved to learner profiles by SpeedyLearning. Do not type a name, address, school, phone number or other private information into the helper.

External resources

Some pictures and fonts are loaded from third-party websites, which can receive ordinary web requests. Offline lessons only cache SpeedyLearning's own educational files.

Your choices

Settings let you turn optional learning recommendations, anonymous activity totals and AI assistance on or off. To remove learning records, a grown-up can delete a child's login from Grown-ups: manage logins, use the progress reset in My Progress, or clear this site's browser storage for progress kept only on this device.
