🎮 You're the pilot

Computer: W A S D or arrow keys to fly in any direction. Space = Pulse, Shift = Dash, P = Pause.

Phone or tablet: use the four direction buttons, or touch the world to guide the spaceship. Press PULSE or DASH to activate your abilities.

Controller: left stick to steer, A = Pulse, B = Dash.

Find the crystal that answers the question in the mission display. Each answer unlocks the next rift. Touch golden stars for extra points. Drones can bump you, but there is no game over.

Accessibility and comfort settings

Sound is off by default; Calm view reduces visual movement. The game can be played using the keyboard, large touch buttons, a controller, or the answer buttons. Maths scores stay on this device. No accounts or chat with strangers.
