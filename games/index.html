🎮 How to play · For pupils and grown-ups

1. Choose your year group. 2. Read the maths mission. 3. Select up to three numbered crystals to make your answer — press a crystal again to remove it. Several different combinations can be correct. 4. Use your school method or the 💬 speech bubble on the main SpeedyLearning page if you need more help. 5. Ignite the engine. 6. Fly the lightship using up/down, a gamepad or touch movement. Collect lights, without a timer or penalty, then move to the next mission.

Accessibility: keyboard numbers 1–9 select crystals, Backspace removes your last, Enter ignites, H gives a hint, arrows steer, P pauses. With a gamepad, use the D-pad or left stick to pick crystals, A to choose, B to undo, X to ignite, Y for a hint; use the left stick in flight and Start to pause. Turn off sound or use Calm view at the top. The mathematical choices have ordinary buttons even if the animated world cannot be displayed. Progress is stored only on this device.
